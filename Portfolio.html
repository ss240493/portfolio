Playable Horror Micro-Game

Midnight Corridor

Press Start to enter.

Survive the corridor.

Collect 3 keys, unlock the exit, and avoid the Shade. Use WASD or arrow keys to move.

Objectives

  • Find all 3 keys.
  • Reach the exit door.
  • Don’t let the Shade reach you.

Controls

Move: WASD / Arrow keys

Pause: Spacebar

Restart: R
